# Privacy Policy for MySkedul

Last updated: [12/04/2026]

## Introduction

MySkedul respects your privacy. This app is designed to help you manage your schedule efficiently without collecting unnecessary personal data.

---

## Information We Collect

MySkedul does **not collect or store personal information on external servers**.

The app may store the following data locally on your device:
- Schedules and timetable data
- Tasks and notes
- App preferences

This data remains on your device unless you choose to share it.

---

## Cloud Sharing Feature

MySkedul allows users to share schedules using a unique code.

- Shared data is limited to schedule-related content
- No sensitive personal information is required
- Data is only shared when the user initiates the action

---

## Notifications

MySkedul may send local notifications to:
- Remind you about classes
- Alert you about updates

These notifications are generated on your device and do not require personal data.

---

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. Since most data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it.

---

## Third-Party Services

MySkedul may use third-party services (such as hosting platforms) for features like updates or sharing. These services may have their own privacy policies.

---

## Your Control

You can:
- Delete your data anytime by clearing app data
- Control permissions like notifications from device settings

---

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
